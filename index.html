Playback

Leave this at zero unless you know why you are moving it. It shifts playback only — never the measurement or verdict — but a synthesised ring an octave off the real one is the fastest way to talk yourself into a match that is not there. The panel turns red whenever it is non-zero. Phone speaker response varies. Use headphones for quiet playback; stop playback before measuring a strike.

Comparison

Your planchet, wrong metal — mass follows density, so the comparison shows what the same disc would weigh in that alloy. It follows the coin under test until you point it somewhere else; set it back to the same coin and it starts following again. The interval is worth learning by ear, but the mass line underneath it is what actually catches the coin.

A/B playback holds planchet dimensions fixed and changes alloy; the displayed mass follows density. It is an illustration, not the candidate-alloy test used in the result. Validate predictions using measured genuine specimens before relying on them.

Start a test, allow the microphone, then strike the coin about 15 cm away. Keep support and distance unchanged for all three strikes.

Three usable strikes per test. Provisional repeatability limit: 1% total frequency spread.

Each capture must pass clipping, length, tonal prominence and pre-strike noise checks. Rejected captures do not count toward the three strikes. Wait for the ring to stop before striking again. Two distinct upper modes must recur in all three strikes for an acoustic verdict.

The three-strike result freezes for review. A nearby coin or speaker can still supply the sound: isolate your specimen. A reported sample rate does not establish the microphone's actual bandwidth. Loaded files need one strike, at least 60 ms of quiet before it, and half a second after it.

Dimensions and provenance

Wear allowance is a provisional downward pitch tolerance for catalogue geometry only. It is ignored when the relevant mass or thickness is confirmed measured, to avoid counting the same loss twice. Relief and rim changes are not represented by a flat-disc model.

Use your own figures, not the catalogue's. Thickness is what the model needs; mass is one way to get it, calipers are the other. Switch below if you are working without a scale.

Elastic constants

Modulus is the largest single uncertainty in every absolute frequency here, roughly ±8%. Mode ratios are untouched by it.

Measured Q is diagnostic only. Decay needs an R² of at least 0.90 and an 8 dB span to be reported. Material Q above controls synthesized playback, not the verdict.
